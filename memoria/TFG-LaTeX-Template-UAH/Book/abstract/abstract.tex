\chapter*{Abstract}
\label{cha:abstract}

\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

In this BsC Thesis, the ability of different unsupervised machine learning models to separate temporal signals caused by noise sources from those generated by photons has been explored. The objective is to identify a model that outperforms the classification capacity of the Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) used in the article that served as the starting point for this work.To address this task, four different models have been trained with both types of signals (captured by the TES detector). Initially, the nature of the signals was analyzed in order to apply the corresponding preprocessing techniques. Once the models were trained, they were evaluated under the same metrics, with the purpose of comparing them and determining the model with the best noise-discriminative performance.
